SURGICAL PATHOLOGY REPORT

SPECIMEN: Right breast, lumpectomy with sentinel lymph node biopsy.
CLINICAL HISTORY: Palpable mass, 2 cm, upper outer quadrant. Screening mammogram BI-RADS 4C.

MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION:
Sections show an invasive ductal carcinoma measuring 2.1 cm in greatest dimension, with moderate nuclear pleomorphism and a mitotic rate of 8 per 10 high-power fields. Lymphovascular invasion is present at the periphery of the tumor. All inked surgical margins are negative for carcinoma; the closest margin is 4 mm at the deep aspect. Two sentinel lymph nodes were identified and are negative for metastatic carcinoma (0/2) by routine H&E and cytokeratin immunohistochemistry.

IMMUNOHISTOCHEMISTRY:
Estrogen receptor: positive, 90% of tumor nuclei, strong intensity.
Progesterone receptor: positive, 60% of tumor nuclei, moderate intensity.
HER2 by immunohistochemistry: score 2+, equivocal. Reflex FISH testing is pending.

FINAL DIAGNOSIS:
1. Invasive ductal carcinoma, right breast, 2.1 cm, margins negative.
2. Lymphovascular invasion present.
3. Sentinel lymph nodes negative (0/2).
4. Estrogen and progesterone receptor positive; HER2 equivocal, FISH pending.
